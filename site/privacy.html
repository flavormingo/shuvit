Privacy Policy

Last updated November 1, 2026

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, display name and avatar, if you create an account.
  • What you contribute: photos, videos, descriptions and tags you submit, and the favorites you save.
  • Safety records: reports you file and users you block.
  • Technical logs: IP addresses and requests, kept in server logs to run and protect the service.

You can browse parks without an account.

Your location

With your permission, the app uses your location on your device to center the map and show how far away parks are. Your precise location isn't sent to us or stored. To load parks, the app sends us the area of the map you're viewing.

Photos and videos

We remove embedded metadata, including GPS location, from uploads when we process them. Contributions are reviewed before they're published. Once approved, they're public, along with your display name, avatar and contribution count.

How we use your data

We use it only to run Shuvit: signing you in, showing contributions, moderating content and keeping the service secure. We don't sell your data, show ads or track you across other apps and websites.

Who else handles it

  • Resend delivers our sign-in and notification emails.
  • Apple provides maps in the app. Apple's own privacy policy applies to that.

Park locations come from OpenStreetMap. We don't send them your personal data.

Keeping and deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time in the app under Profile > Delete Account. This immediately and permanently removes your account, favorites, contributions and their files from the live service. Copies can remain in access-restricted backups for up to 60 days before they're removed.

Children

Shuvit isn't intended for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above. Questions or requests: hi@shuvit.app.
